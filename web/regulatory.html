Water-activity rules

§ A · 1–2
01
P0

水域遊憩活動管理辦法 — Water Recreation Activity Management Regulations

Governing · 交通部觀光署 County delegation · yes Last amended · 2024-04-16

Issued under Article 36 of 發展觀光條例. The 辦法 lists specific activities (SUP, diving, jet-ski, free-diving) and delegates day-to-day supervision to municipalities and counties under Article 4. When Yilan, Hualien, or Pingtung designates 溯溪 locally, its general safety provisions — operator insurance (Art. 9), safety briefings (Art. 10) — apply.

Open · verify Yilan county ordinance coverage

02
P0

辦理溯溪活動應注意事項 — River-tracing Operational Notes

Governing · 運動部 (ex-體育署) Ratio · 1 guide : 5 clients Pre-filing · 14 days

This is the operative instrument for 溯溪, issued under 教育部. It sets the 1:5 guide-to-client ratio, mandatory gear (helmet, PFD, river-tracing shoes, GPS, comms), a 14-day pre-activity filing with the county, and recognises either a 山域嚮導 溯溪-category holder or a qualified "安全人員" (including foreign-body certificate holders) as a valid guide.

山域嚮導 — 溯溪類 — legally-recognised canyoning guide

Legal basis · 山域嚮導資格檢定辦法 Training · ≥48 hrs Cost · NT$25–45k

At least one Taiwanese founder must hold this before launch. Requires 18+, clean record, physical exam, BLS/CPR. Written + practical exam. 2025 exam sessions: 4/11, 7/11, 10/31. Stricter 1:5 ratio under 注意事項 governs over the 1:8 in the 檢定辦法.

有限公司 registration — limited-liability company

Recommended capital · NT$500k Fees · NT$5–15k Timeline · 2–4 weeks

No statutory minimum capital, but NT$500k is the threshold for a foreign-manager work permit — useful later if Duncan applies. Adventure-liability exposure rules out 行號 (unlimited personal liability) despite the smaller budget.

FIA approval — Foreign Investment Approval (投資審議司)

Authority · MOEA · DIR Filing fee · NT$0 Timeline · 2–6 wks

US founders can own 100% of a 有限公司 and can serve as shareholder/director with no TW residency required. Triggers FIA before capital can be registered. Consider structuring Duncan under 1/3 ownership to simplify work-permit implications later.

Travel-agency license

§ E · 9–10
09
P2

旅行業執照 (甲種) — inbound-licensed travel agency

Paid-in capital · NT$6M Bond · NT$1.5M Applies to us · NO

Class A (甲種) licence enables direct inbound sales to foreign tourists. 60× our capital budget, therefore out of scope. Class B (乙種, NT$1.2M) is domestic-only and does not help us reach foreigners. 丙種 does not exist in current regulation.

Licensed-agency partnership — the standard workaround

Commission · 15–25% Insurance · covered by partner Model · activity product on OTA

11
P1

County / national-park / indigenous permits

Yilan · easiest Hualien · post-quake restrictions Pingtung · Rukai consultation

Ranking for a new entrant: Yilan (Nanao) → Pingtung (Maolin) → Hualien. Nanao has the established commercial 溯溪 corridor. 哈盆 is a Nature Reserve — commercial tours generally prohibited. Maolin requires 6–12 months of Rukai community relationship-building before paid ops. Taroko proper is still staged-reopen post-2024 earthquake.

Verify 鹿皮溪 + 金岳 trailhead land status — 林班地 or 原住民保留地?

§ Open questions · lawyer brief

Ten questions
for the lawyer call.

Pay them once, ask ten things. Prioritised by how much each answer unblocks.

  1. Q · 01 Exact boundary: at what point does a "溯溪 experience" become "旅行業" under 發展觀光條例 Art. 27? Hotel pickup? Bundled meals? Multi-day?
  2. Q · 02 FIA: is tourism/recreation still on the foreign-investment negative list, or is this a simple notification?
  3. Q · 03 Does the 運動部 "山域嚮導" regime preempt 交通部觀光署 water-activity filing for 溯溪, or do both apply?
  4. Q · 04 Has Yilan formally designated 溯溪 as a managed activity under Art. 4 of 水域遊憩活動管理辦法? Any supplemental county rules?
  5. Q · 05 Does Duncan's foreign cert (Rescue 3 SRT + Red Cross) qualify him as 安全人員 on our trips without a TW 山域嚮導?
  6. Q · 06 Which carrier writes 公共意外責任保險 covering 溯溪 without rider exclusions at NT$5M / NT$10M?
  7. Q · 07 Template contract terms for listing as a product on KKday / Klook — liability flow, insurance claim path.
  8. Q · 08 Nanao land: are 鹿皮溪 and 金岳 trailheads on 林班地 or 原住民保留地? Agreements needed?
  9. Q · 09 GUI / 統一發票 issuance when foreign-tourist bookings route through partner agencies vs. direct.
  10. Q · 10 If TW founders wear "guide" and "shareholder" hats simultaneously, any 勞基法 labor-law exposure on trip days?
