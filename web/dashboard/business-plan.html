Open wedge市場缺口

The NT$4,500–5,500 half-day slot with Taipei pickup, English + Japanese delivery, and instant-book online is empty. Commodity operators cluster at NT$2,200–2,400 with form-based inquiry. Technical canyoning (JUST, Shawa) sits at NT$4,800+ but reads Chinese-first. Realistic defensive window before a funded incumbent copies it: 12–24 months.NT$4,500–5,500 的半日遊定位 — 含台北接送、英日文服務、即時線上訂購 — 目前市場空缺。低價業者集中在 NT$2,200–2,400 且須填表詢問。技術性峽谷(JUST、Shawa)約 NT$4,800+,但以中文為主。被有資金的業者複製之前的合理防禦窗口:12–24 個月。

Physical capacity limits this to a 150–250 trip-pax / year business with pricing power. Not a volume play. A lifestyle-plus shape, not a venture shape.實體產能上限約 150–250 人次/年,具定價力,非走量模式,是生活方式型事業,非創投型事業。

Why not BnB first為何不先做民宿

A Nanao basecamp is the most emotionally compelling second stream but the wrong first move. 民宿 license requires owner-occupancy (no outsourced manager), rent path needs NT$1.5–2M cash before opening, buy path NT$10–14M. Illegal-Airbnb fines run NT$100k–2M. No small TW guiding startup has succeeded by leading with lodging as Phase 2. Reserved as Year 3 gate-only.南澳基地在情感上是最吸引人的第二條收入,但不是正確的第一步。民宿執照需業主自住(不能委外管理),租屋路線開業前需 NT$150–200 萬,買房路線需 NT$1,000–1,400 萬。非法 Airbnb 罰款 NT$10–200 萬。沒有小型台灣嚮導新創以住宿作為第二階段而成功。保留為第三年決策門。

The real Year 2 unlock:第二年真正的突破點: corporate team-building (weekday utilization, 2–3× per-pax price, lower weather risk) and B2B whitelabel (operate river-tracing trips on behalf of licensed travel agencies — their sales, our guiding). Both break the guide-hour ceiling without capital.企業團建(平日利用率、每人價格提升 2–3 倍、天氣風險較低)與 B2B 白標(替持牌旅行社執行溯溪行程 — 他們銷售、我們帶隊)。兩者皆可在不需資本的情況下突破帶隊時數上限。
