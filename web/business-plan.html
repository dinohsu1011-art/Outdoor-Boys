Open wedge

The NT$4,500–5,500 half-day slot with Taipei pickup, English + Japanese delivery, and instant-book online is empty. Commodity operators cluster at NT$2,200–2,400 with form-based inquiry. Technical canyoning (JUST, Shawa) sits at NT$4,800+ but reads Chinese-first. Realistic defensive window before a funded incumbent copies it: 12–24 months.

Physical capacity limits this to a 150–250 trip-pax / year business with pricing power. Not a volume play. A lifestyle-plus shape, not a venture shape.

What the math says

Break-even ~16 trips / yr on Year 2 cost base. Hit by June of Year 2. One full-time founder salary absorbable in Year 3; two full-time salaries need Year 4+ or a second SKU (corporate team-building, B2B whitelabel) or a second base.

Cash gap. Year 1 modeled cost is NT$162k against a headline NT$100k budget. Add paid-in capital commitment and realistic buffer and true founding cash need is NT$800–900k, not NT$600k. Close via founder pro-rata top-up or by delaying entity / insurance to Q4 2026.

Why not BnB first

A Nanao basecamp is the most emotionally compelling second stream but the wrong first move. 民宿 license requires owner-occupancy (no outsourced manager), rent path needs NT$1.5–2M cash before opening, buy path NT$10–14M. Illegal-Airbnb fines run NT$100k–2M. No small TW guiding startup has succeeded by leading with lodging as Phase 2. Reserved as Year 3 gate-only.

The real Year 2 unlock: corporate team-building (weekday utilization, 2–3× per-pax price, lower weather risk) and B2B whitelabel (operate river-tracing trips on behalf of licensed travel agencies — their sales, our guiding). Both break the guide-hour ceiling without capital.
