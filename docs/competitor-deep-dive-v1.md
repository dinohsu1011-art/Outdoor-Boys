## 1. Canyons Taiwan — https://www.canyonstaiwan.com/

### What it is
An English-first Taiwan canyoning & river-tracing **editorial + booking concierge**, not an operating guide outfit. It self-describes the booking service as *"Currently, the service is free"* — it takes inquiries via a form and routes them to partner guides. ([booking page](https://www.canyonstaiwan.com/book-canyoning-tour/))

### Offering
- **Activities:** canyoning + river tracing, classic day trips and multi-day expeditions
- **Named routes in editorial:** Weijin Creek (Hualien), Deer Antler Creek, Qizhongtian Waterfall Group, Yuemeikeng, Luoshan Waterfall ([classic day trips page](https://www.canyonstaiwan.com/classic-canyoning-day-trips-taiwan/))
- **Pricing tiers** quoted on booking page (note: in **USD/EUR**, not TWD):
  - Beginner 4-6hr — **$170-230 / €150-200**
  - Intermediate 6-8hr — **$230-347 / €200-300**
  - Expert 8+hr — variable
- **Partner guides explicitly named:**
  - **JUST Canyoning** — "specializes in northern Taiwan"
  - **Shark Band** — "river and rock tours in northern Taiwan and Hualien"

### Distribution & brand
- Own site only — **no KKday / Klook listings**
- Booking form fields: activity, difficulty, region, date, pax count, experience
- English-language throughout. No ZH mirror visible.
- Standout feature: a **Taiwan River Accident Database** with translated reports and a topographic-map resource library ([accident db](https://www.canyonstaiwan.com/taiwan-river-accident-database/)). That's the real moat — editorial authority, not guide ops.

### Our read
- **Do NOT try to beat it as a content portal** in Year 1 — years of SEO already accrued.
- **Do become one of its partner guides in Yilan/Nan'ao.** It already routes JUST Canyoning (northern) and Shark Band (Hualien). Yilan is a whitespace on its map.
- If we can get on the partner list, that's a **qualified-lead pipeline** with no CAC. Outreach priority: Q2 2026 once we have one insured pilot trip under our belt.
- Also: copy the accident-database idea as a **vertical-integrated safety artifact** specific to our routes — not portal-wide, but route-by-route pre-trip briefings. Customers will value it, and insurers may price the policy down.

## 2. JUST Canyoning (JCI) — https://justcanyoning.org/

### What it is
A dual-play operator: (a) **commercial canyoning tours** (Taiwan / Japan / China), (b) **Taiwan Canyoning Association (TCA)** training institute running V1–V4 progression courses + a bolting workshop. Dual domain: `.org` English, `.com` Chinese. ([about](https://justcanyoning.org/about/), [training](https://justcanyoning.org/canyoning-training/))

### Base & principal
- **Address:** No. 59, Lane 29, Fenglin Rd, Tamsui District, Taipei ([contact](https://justcanyoning.org/contact/))
- **Principal / instructor:** Justen Wu (per external-source triangulation; not verified on the site we fetched). Training materials reference instructors "certified with the Taiwan Canyoning Association (TCA), incorporating methodologies from CIC, AIGC, CGI."

Pricing is gated behind inquiry. Instructor cert lineage: CIC (Commission Internationale de Canyon), AIGC (Italian Canyon Guide Assn), TCA, CGI ([training](https://justcanyoning.org/canyoning-training/)). No explicit 2026 course calendar published.

### Distribution & brand
- Booking via WhatsApp / Facebook / Instagram / contact form — **no instant-book**, no KKday/Klook listings
- Languages: **English & Mandarin** (per training page)
- No Japanese offer visible
- Countries: Taiwan, Japan, China

### Our read
- **Strongest moat:** international cert lineage (CIC/AIGC) + in-house training pipeline feeding the trip arm. We cannot replicate this in Year 1.
- **Biggest weaknesses:** no instant-book anywhere, no Japanese, dense-prose site, Tamsui base is not beachfront-adjacent for foreign tourists.
- **Partnership posture:** JCI is a plausible **certification partner for Dino and Luke** once we've completed Sports-Admin 溯溪類 baseline — V1–V2 would put us on an international cert track without having to travel to Italy or France.
- **Competitive posture:** don't try to compete on deep-canyon technical expertise (they win). Compete on: (a) instant-book online, (b) Yilan/Nan'ao base, (c) Japanese language, (d) river-tracing-first product (they lean canyoning).
- **Pricing anchor:** we should enter at **€100-150 / NT$3,500-5,000 half-day**, undercutting JCI's beginner tier by ~25% while still well above Klook's mass-market NT$1,800-2,500 floor. Premium positioning, accessible entry price.

## 5. Cross-cutting synthesis

| Operator | Routes / region | Price band (half-day pp) | Channel | Languages | Booking UX |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Canyons Taiwan | concierge, all TW | €150-300 quoted | own form → partners | EN | slow (form + email) |
| JUST Canyoning | Taipei-north + Kaohsiung | €150-250 | own form / WhatsApp / IG | EN + ZH | slow |
| 4Season | n/a (training only) | n/a | — | ZH | n/a |
| Kiki/台灣溪降協會 | Hualien (presumed) | unknown | FB only | ZH (presumed) | unknown |

### What this tells us — 8 tactical implications

1. **No one sells instant-book.** Every named competitor routes to a form or a DM. Shipping a proper online checkout = immediate differentiation.
2. **No one sells in Japanese.** ~2M annual Japanese visitors to TW. Apple's edge is structural, not cosmetic.
3. **No one owns Yilan/Nan'ao in English.** JCI is north-Taipei, Shark Band is Hualien, Canyons Taiwan has no Yilan partner. This confirms the `scope-roadmap-v1.md` base recommendation.
4. **Premium floor is ~€150 / NT$5,000 half-day.** We should price at ~NT$3,500-5,000 half-day — below JCI, 2× the KKday mass floor. "Accessible premium."
5. **Canyons Taiwan is a distribution channel, not a moat.** Get on its partner list by Q2/Q3 2026.
6. **4Season is the cert pipeline.** Lock the 2026 course calendar by end of April.
7. **Route-level editorial is undervalued.** Canyons Taiwan's accident DB is lonely. We can ship per-route pre-trip safety briefings as a standard deliverable — brand asset and insurance-friendly.
8. **Avoid technical-canyoning combat with JCI.** Lead with river tracing. Canyoning is a Year-2 SKU, layered in after we have V1-V2 certs from JCI itself.

### Partnership candidates (rank-ordered)

1. **4Season** — cert pipeline · outreach Q1 2026
2. **Canyons Taiwan** — inbound-lead pipeline · outreach Q2 2026 after first insured pilot
3. **JCI** — cert pipeline (V1-V2) and plausible referral for canyoning overflow · outreach Q3 2026

### Acquisition / acquihire watchlist

- If "Kiki" page turns out to be a solo Hualien guide winding down, that's a potential 1-guide acquihire for Year 2 Hualien entry. Low cost. Low confidence this is available — investigate via FB login first.
