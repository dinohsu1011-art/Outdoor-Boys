### 1. CPR / BLS

1. **Official name:** Basic Life Support / 基本救命術 (BLS)
2. **Issuing org:** 中華民國紅十字會 [redcross.org.tw](https://www.redcross.org.tw); AHA-affiliated hospitals also offer BLS-Provider courses.
3. **Testing body / location:** Local Red Cross branches (Taipei, Yilan, Taichung, etc.). Rolling enrollment on [redcross-class.org.tw](https://www.redcross-class.org.tw/ClassInfo).
4. **Schedule:** Monthly classes in most cities. Check portal above for 2026 dates — no fixed annual calendar.
5. **Cost:** ~NT$1,500–2,500, includes card fee.
6. **Duration:** 8 hrs (1 day), written + skills station.
7. **Prerequisites:** None.
8. **Difficulty:** Very high pass rate; skills-based check-off. No physical test.
9. **Renewal:** 2 years ([Red Cross TW training portal](https://www.redcross-class.org.tw/ClassInfo)).
10. **Assigned:** All four founders. Prerequisite for 山域嚮導 检定 (see Tier 1).

### 2. 紅十字會 水上安全救生員 (Water Safety Lifeguard)

1. **Official name:** 水上安全救生員 / Water Safety Lifeguard
2. **Issuing org:** 中華民國紅十字會 [redcross.org.tw](https://www.redcross.org.tw); alternative: 中華民國水上救生協會 [ctwlsa.org.tw](http://ctwlsa.org.tw/b101.php).
3. **Testing body / location:** Red Cross branches run the course; 體育署 certifies the exam. Courses available in Taipei, Taichung, Kaohsiung, occasionally Yilan. Pool venues vary.
4. **Schedule:** Multiple cohorts per year (spring / summer / fall). No single national calendar — check [redcross-class.org.tw](https://www.redcross-class.org.tw/ClassInfo) and the Taipei Water Safety Volunteer Association [tpewlsa.org.tw](https://www.tpewlsa.org.tw/how-to-be-a-lifeguard/) for 2026 slots.
5. **Cost:** NT$4,500 (台大 class, bare-bones) to NT$7,500 (TPE Water Safety Volunteer Association, includes cert fee + gear + pool + insurance + meals) [[tpewlsa.org.tw](https://www.tpewlsa.org.tw/how-to-be-a-lifeguard/)]. Plus 體育署 exam fees: 學科 NT$500 + 術科 NT$1,000 + 證照規費 NT$200 [[sportsv.net Q&A](https://www.sportsv.net/articles/42195)].
6. **Duration:** ~85 hours across ~19 days (evenings + weekends typical).
7. **Prerequisites:** 18+, basic swimming competence (must pass entry swim test in most programs).
8. **Difficulty:** Moderate-to-hard. Entry swim test commonly **100m 捷泳 + 100m 蛙式 within 5 min (M) / 6 min (F)** [[sportsv.net](https://www.sportsv.net/articles/42195)]. Skills exam includes victim rescue, tow, spinal immobilization in water. Repeats are allowed.
9. **Renewal:** Valid 3 years, renewable via a shorter re-cert course.
10. **Assigned:** Dino, Luke, Apple in Year 1 (sequence them through three different cohorts to spread cash + keep one founder available). Duncan deferred — he will hold Rescue 3 SRT which is superior anyway; he can do TW 救生員 after relocation in Year 2.

### 3. Wilderness First Aid (WFA, 16hr)

1. **Official name:** Wilderness First Aid (WFA) — the 16-hour version is the industry entry-level.
2. **Issuing org:** Imagine Taiwan (WMAI-certified) [imagine-taiwan.com/firstaid](https://imagine-taiwan.com/firstaid/); Taiwan Adventures runs the SOLO curriculum [taiwan-adventures.com WFA](http://www.taiwan-adventures.com/events/wfa-wilderness-first-aid-course/); NOLS runs periodic WFAs via Crossing Latitudes in Taoyuan [crossinglatitudes.com](https://www.crossinglatitudes.com/nols-course/wfa-taiwan-2025) (2025 was their most recent TW offering; **check whether 2026 TW dates are posted**).
3. **Testing body / location:** Taipei (Imagine Taiwan), Taoyuan (NOLS via Crossing Latitudes), occasional field venues. No location near Yilan — founders will travel.
4. **Schedule:** Imagine Taiwan runs quarterly; Taiwan Adventures runs ~2x/year. **No 2026 dates visible in public search results — email the orgs.**
5. **Cost:** Imagine Taiwan WFA 1-day = ~NT$6,000–9,000 including materials + WMAI card [[imagine-taiwan.com](https://imagine-taiwan.com/firstaid/)]. NOLS WFA USD $295–395 when run in TW.
6. **Duration:** 16 hrs (2 days) is standard; Imagine Taiwan offers a compressed 8-hour variant but the 16-hour version is preferred for guide work.
7. **Prerequisites:** None. CPR recommended but not required (often bundled).
8. **Difficulty:** High pass rate; scenario-based practical. No physical test.
9. **Renewal:** 2 years.
10. **Assigned:** All four founders. Do this as a group offsite — shared scenario practice is high team-building value.

### 1. 山域嚮導 — 溯溪類 (Mountain Guide — Canyoning/River-Tracing, Dino)

1. **Official name:** 山域嚮導（溯溪類）— 溪降專長 / Mountain Guide, Canyoning Category, Canyon-Descent Specialty.
2. **Issuing org:** 運動部 (Ministry of Sports; previously 教育部體育署). Legal basis: 山域嚮導資格檢定辦法. Central registry on i-運動資訊平台 [isports.sa.gov.tw](https://isports.sa.gov.tw/Apps/MTG/Index.aspx).
3. **Testing body / location:** 運動部-accredited training providers run the training + first-stage exam. **Adventure Taiwan (艾格探險)** is the dominant commercial provider and has been a 運動部 training unit since 2019 [[adventuretaiwan.com](https://www.adventuretaiwan.com/mountain-guide-program/)]. They run courses from Taipei with practical sessions in northern TW rivers. Final 運動部 exam administered at designated locations.
4. **Schedule:** 2025 written-exam sittings were **4/11, 7/11, 10/31** per Adventure Taiwan. **2026 dates not yet posted in public search results** — check [canyoning-guide page](https://www.adventuretaiwan.com/mountain-guide-program/canyoning-guide/) directly. Expect similar spring/summer/fall cadence.
5. **Cost breakdown** [[adventuretaiwan.com canyoning-guide-training](https://www.adventuretaiwan.com/product/canyoning-guide-training/)]:
   - Training fee (Adventure Taiwan 6-day program, 2 stages): ~NT$28,000–35,000 standard; NT$20,800 student rate.
   - 運動部 exam fees [[isports.sa.gov.tw Q&A](https://isports.sa.gov.tw/Apps/Fessay.aspx?SYS=MTG&PKNO=401&MENU_PRG_CD=95)]: 學科 NT$1,000 + 術科 NT$3,000 + 證書費 NT$300 = **NT$4,300**.
   - Personal gear (harness, helmet, wetsuit if not already owned): NT$15,000+ out of pocket.
   - **Total excluding gear:** ~NT$32,000–40,000.
6. **Duration:** ≥48 hrs regulatory minimum; Adventure Taiwan's 溪降 program is 6 days across 2 stages, 4–9 participants per cohort.
7. **Prerequisites:** 18+ (minors need guardian consent); valid BLS/CPR cert (Tier 0 #1); 良民證 (police clearance); medical clearance exam. 水上救生員 not strictly required by law but strongly expected by insurers.
8. **Difficulty:** Written pass mark 70 (80% from the iSports question bank, 20% from course content). Practical exam has two stages — technical skills + leading-team skills — each scored to 70. Pass rates not published but anecdotally Adventure Taiwan cohorts pass the first written attempt at a high rate when students actually prep the question bank.
9. **Renewal:** **3 years**, via continuing-education requirements + activity record [[isports.sa.gov.tw](https://isports.sa.gov.tw/Apps/MTG/Index.aspx)].
10. **Assigned:** **Dino.** This is the single legally-required cert for the business — P0. Target completion before first paid trip.

### 4. 華語導遊人員 / Support-guide legal minimum (Apple)

**The honest answer: there is no single "support guide" cert mandated by 辦理溯溪活動應注意事項.** The regulation requires (a) at least one 山域嚮導（溯溪類）holder as lead and (b) a 1:5 guide-to-client ratio. Secondary people on the trip are "assistants" / porters / 安全人員 and the regulation does not impose a specific license on them — but insurance carriers and partner agencies (KKday / Klook) typically ask for documented water-rescue training on every on-trip staff member.

**Apple's pragmatic stack:**
- Tier 0 (CPR + 水上救生員 + WFA) covers the insurance / platform diligence.
- **Optional but recommended: 華語導遊人員執照** (Chinese-language tour guide license, 交通部觀光署). Not required for our business model (we do not hold 旅行業執照), but it is the *de facto* credential a KKday listing expects on the profile photo and it lets Apple legally narrate and lead foreign-tourist groups in hospitality contexts outside the activity itself (e.g., if we ever add a cultural pre-day).
  - **Issuing org:** 交通部觀光署 via 導遊與領隊評量系統 [register-exam.tad.gov.tw](https://register-exam.tad.gov.tw/tourism/Login).
  - **Next written exam:** **2026-04-29** for Chinese-language guide; foreign-language oral **2026-05-24–25** [[tad.gov.tw](https://admin.taiwan.net.tw/BusinessInfo/FilePage?a=146)].
  - **Cost:** Exam fee + ~NT$7,000 training for new trainees post-2024 reform [[register-exam.tad.gov.tw](https://register-exam.tad.gov.tw/tourism/ExamQandAh)].
  - **Renewal:** Valid indefinitely once issued, subject to continuing-ed hours.

**Japanese-speaking angle:** Apple is our strongest Japanese speaker. The **外語導遊（日語）** track is higher-value than 華語 if inbound Japanese market matters — it requires oral proficiency exam but permits legal Japanese-language guiding. **Defer to Year 2** decision once we see channel mix.

**Assigned:** **Apple — Tier 0 stack only for Year 1.** 華語導遊 only if Apr-29 exam timing is feasible alongside his 救生員 training; otherwise 2027.
