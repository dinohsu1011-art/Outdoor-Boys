## Vision

Outdoor Boys is a Taiwan-based adventure guiding company that brings foreign visitors into Taiwan's rivers and canyons for world-class day trips they can't get anywhere else. We believe Taiwan's geography — a subtropical island with 200+ peaks over 3,000m, thousands of rivers cutting slot canyons through the Central Mountain Range, and most of it 90 minutes from a major city — is the single most underrated adventure destination in Asia. Our job is to make it accessible to English-speaking travelers through safe, well-run day trips booked online in English.

## What we're building (Year 1)

**In scope:**
- Day trips only (no multi-day, no packaged itineraries)
- River tracing (溯溪) and canyoning as the core offering
- Target customer: English-speaking inbound foreign tourists
- Secondary: expats living in TW, and Japanese-speaking tourists (Apple's wedge)
- Self-booked online in English
- One geographic base in Year 1 (decision pending — see below)

**Explicitly NOT in scope (Year 1):**
- Multi-day expeditions
- Mountain / alpine climbing (regulatory complexity, different skill set)
- Packaged flights-to-finish itineraries (triggers travel-agency licensing we can't afford)
- Chinese-speaking domestic market (saturated; not our edge)
- Gear retail / e-commerce

## Geography decision (pending)

Three candidates. Final call after regulatory research lands.

| Base | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| **Yilan** (宜蘭) | 2 hr from Taipei (biggest inbound airport). Strong 溯溪 rivers — 南澳南溪, 哈盆越嶺, 梵梵溪. Lower regulatory complexity than national park land. Luke/Apple commutable from Taipei. | Summer-seasonal; typhoon risk. |
| **Hualien** (花蓮) | Brand halo — Taroko is world-famous. Deep canyoning potential in 砂婆礑, 翡翠谷, 慕谷慕魚. | Taroko is a national park → 入山證 + park permits; higher friction. Further from Taipei. 2024 earthquake recovery still ongoing in parts. |
| **Pingtung / Kaohsiung** (茂林) | Close to Kaohsiung airport. Year-round warmer weather. Less competition from existing operators. | Indigenous land requires 魯凱族 consultation. Smaller inbound traffic than Taipei. |

**Current lean:** Yilan. Proximity to Taipei (where 80%+ of inbound foreign visitors land) is the deciding factor. Subject to regulatory research.

## Positioning hypothesis

Hypothesis (to validate with competitive scan):

> The market has decent Chinese-language 溯溪 operators and a handful of English-speaking adventure outfitters, but almost no one is running a genuinely digital-first, English-only booking experience with reliable small-group guarantees. That's our wedge.

Concretely:
- **English-only website**, no awkward auto-translated Chinese.
- **Book in 60 seconds** — Klook/KKday level UX on our own site, no Facebook DM required.
- **Small-group guarantee** — max 8 guests per guide.
- **Taipei pickup option** — shuttle from downtown Taipei for +NT$1,000.
- **Japanese-language trip option** — Apple-led. Underserved.

## Kill criteria

We stop or pivot if any of these hit in Year 1:
1. Licensing path for foreign-tourist day trips requires >NT$3M capital and no viable partnership workaround exists.
2. Insurance for 溯溪 operators with <2 years of operating history is unavailable or >NT$100k/year premium.
3. After 10 expat trial trips in Q3, NPS is below 30 or we have any serious safety incident.
4. None of us can pass 溯溪嚮導 certification within 2 attempts.
