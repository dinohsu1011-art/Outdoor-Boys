# Outdoor Boys — Feasibility Study

`v1 · 21 April 2026 · drafted by planning`
`status: internal decision document`

## Executive verdict

**MODIFIED-GO.** The market, regulatory path, and operational model are feasible — but the current roadmap understates the true cash need, overloads a part-time four-founder team during a short tradable season, and frames Year 1 as "launch" when it is really "certify + dry-run." Recommended path: a compressed **Option B** — friends/expat-only paid trips through 2026, defer the Klook/KKday/foreign-tourist push until certs, insurance, and 20 clean expat runs are banked, then open the inbound channel Q3 2027. Pre-commit ~NT$300–400k of additional founder contributions beyond the stated NT$100k budget or the math does not close.

## 1. Market feasibility

Demand is real and improving. Taiwan inbound recovered to ~7.9M visitors 2024 with a 10M 2025–26 government target ([Taiwan Tourism Administration stats](https://admin.taiwan.net.tw/English/FileUploadCategoryListE003130.aspx)). Japan (~2M) and US (~650k) — our two targets — both cleared pre-COVID highs. TWD weakened from ~28 to ~32 per USD, making NT$5,000 day trips read as ~US$155 to Americans, the psychological sweet spot for a premium day adventure.

**TAM/SAM math (conservative):**
- 8M inbound × 15% take a paid guided activity ≈ 1.2M activity bookings/yr
- English/Japanese adventure-day-trip slice ~3% ≈ 36,000 bookings/yr
- Avg NT$3,500 ≈ **TAM ~NT$126M/yr**
- SAM (Yilan + Taipei-origin, EN/JP, premium tier NT$4k+): ~15% of TAM ≈ **NT$19M/yr**
- 3–5% SAM capture ≈ **NT$570k–950k/yr revenue** — matches the 120–250 trip-pax/yr the team can physically deliver part-time

**Competitive density:** per the scan, English-first operators cluster at NT$2,200–2,400 with form-based inquiry; technical canyoning (Shawa, JUST) sits at NT$4,800+ but reads Chinese-first. The NT$4,500–5,500 English-premium slot with Taipei pickup and Japanese delivery is genuinely empty — defensible 12–24 months before a funded incumbent copies it.

**Verdict: feasible (niche-premium).** Not a volume play. A 150–250 trip-pax/yr business with pricing power is the realistic shape.

## 2. Regulatory feasibility

The regulatory doc's conclusion holds: NT$500k 有限公司 activity provider, skip the NT$6M 甲種 license, distribute via KKday/Klook (they carry 旅行業責任保險 for foreign-tourist bookings), secure one 山域嚮導—溯溪類 cert in-house, file 14-day pre-notifications with Yilan County under 辦理溯溪活動應注意事項.

**Top 3 regulatory kill risks:**
1. **"Activity" vs "旅行業" line:** adding Taipei pickup + lunch + transport bundling (the wedge) could trigger a strict reading of 發展觀光條例 Art. 27 → reclassification as 旅行業 → NT$6M capital requirement → kill. Lawyer-confirm before spending on gear.
2. **Insurance underwriting:** carriers may refuse 溯溪 cover for a sub-2yr operator or require named-insured 嚮導 cert holders. No insurance = no KKday onboarding = no business.
3. **Nanao access politics:** 鹿皮溪 / 金岳 sit on 林班地 and/or 原住民保留地. A single 部落 revocation (precedent: 慕谷慕魚, ~2016) removes the anchor SKU overnight.

**"Activity provider + OTA" legality:** dominant market pattern (Shawa, JUST, RiverKing). Defensible under 旅行業管理規則 Art. 3 only while we sell an experience, not a tour. Self-transport is outside scope; Taipei pickup pushes close to the line. See `regulatory-v1.md` §5.2 open question.

**Duncan FIA risk:** low-to-moderate. TW moved to negative-list FDI; tourism not sensitive ([ICLG FDI Taiwan 2026](https://iclg.com/practice-areas/foreign-direct-investment-regimes-laws-and-regulations/taiwan)). Process drag, not denial. Keep Duncan <1/3 equity; file FIA parallel to entity.

**Verdict: manageable.** Two binary gates (bundled-tour line + insurance bind). Both survivable.

## 4. Team feasibility

**Gaps:**

- **No resident ops manager Tue–Fri daytime.** Luke has a day job; Apple is the natural fit but is titled sales.

**Apple's runway:** unemployed through Q4 2026 at earliest without compensation. **Biggest single team risk.** Best salesperson, only JP channel. Needs: NT$15–20k/month retainer from founder pool OR formal scale-down-to-nights-and-weekends acceptance if Apple takes a job.

**Other skill gaps:**
- No WFR/WFA medical cert. Add to Y1 — NT$12–15k, 2 days. Move to P1.
- 3-language CS coverage across TW business hours — Apple covers daytime ZH/JP; Dino covers EN email on breaks; no live-chat. Accept <12 hr email SLA Y1.

**Verdict: stretched.** Build in 1 quarter of buffer.

## 5. Operational feasibility

**Single-base (Nanao) is correct for Y1.** Matches competitive scan, regulatory doc, and team hour reality.

**Gear logistics:** NT$20k kit needs storage home. Prefer Luke's Yilan-area network (minimal per-trip transport). If unavailable, NT$5k/yr rented locker. Do not plan Taipei→Yilan 2hr haul per trip.

**Customer service:** EN + JP + ZH, distributed team. Apple + Dino carry it; <12 hr email SLA; no live chat promises Y1.

**Weather & refund drain:** 65% tradable-days already baked in. Policy: full refund on operator-cancel (weather/safety), 50% <48h customer-cancel, none <24h. KKday/Klook override for those channels. Realistic drain 3–5% of gross (in processing line).

**Verdict: operable.** CS in 3 languages without full-time ops is the weakest link — breaks if Apple takes a job.

## 7. Kill criteria (quantitative)

1. **Regulatory:** if by end Q2 2026 no TW 旅遊業 lawyer will give a written opinion defending activity-provider model with Taipei pickup, **drop Taipei pickup** (amend, not kill).
2. **Insurance:** if by **30 Sep 2026** no public-liability cover explicitly including 溯溪 binds for < NT$60k/yr, **pause entity filing.**
3. **Certification:** if by **31 Dec 2026** no founder has 山域嚮導—溯溪類, **pause paid operations**; unpaid expat trials continue.
4. **Expat trial quality:** if after 10 expat trips NPS < 30 or any serious safety incident, **do not open foreign-tourist channel**; reassess Q1 2027.
5. **Foreign-tourist traction:** if by **31 Mar 2027** fewer than 10 paid foreign-tourist trips across channels, **pivot to expat + content-first** for +6 months.
6. **Founder runway:** if by **30 Jun 2026** Apple has no retainer or explicit pro-bono commitment through Q4, **restructure roles** before gear spend.
7. **FIA:** if Duncan's FIA denied, **restructure as 3-founder TW entity** with Duncan as US-side IP licensor/contractor, not shareholder.

## 8. Recommended path

**Option B — Modified / compressed.** Defer the "launch as inbound operator" push from April 2027 to ~Q3 2027. Use Q2–Q4 2026 for certs, entity, insurance, **friends-and-expat-only paid trips** at near-cost (NT$2,500/pp, founders unpaid). Use expat trips to (a) seed TripAdvisor/Google reviews, (b) stress-test ops, (c) validate insurance without foreign-tourist liability. Open KKday/Klook only after 20+ expat trips banked + 10+ Google reviews.

**Why not A:** the current roadmap compresses the first revenue-generating season into ~6 months post-entity. NT$100k is materially understated. Launching foreign tourists April 2027 with only 10 Y1 trials and no full season of weather data invites kill-criteria-level incidents.

**Why not C (content-first pivot):** Dino + Duncan have the edge to build a media channel, but outdoor-travel content monetizes in 18–36 months and does not solve the legal/insurance path. Content is a **parallel** workstream (Duncan Q3+), not a substitute. Full C wastes Luke + Apple's comparative advantages.

**Five things we must do differently:**
1. **Raise the real cash target to NT$800–900k** (not NT$600k). Each founder pre-commits NT$50–75k beyond current budget, or formalize a Y1 capital-call clause in the Founders Agreement.
2. **Move the lawyer consultation to April 2026 (this month).** Do not spend NT$35k on cert enrollment before the Taipei-pickup line is lawyer-confirmed.
3. **Retainer for Apple from Q3 2026.** NT$15–20k/month from founder pool.
4. **Start insurance underwriter conversations Q2 2026**, not Q3. Gating constraint.
5. **Reframe "April 2027" as first KKday-channel trip, not first foreign-tourist trip.** Foreign-tourist trips happen Q4 2026 via expat word-of-mouth (unpaid-to-us). Inbound/OTA channel opens after certs + insurance + 20 trial trips.
