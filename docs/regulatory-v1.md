## 1. Water Activity Regulations (水域遊憩活動管理辦法)

**Governing law:** 水域遊憩活動管理辦法, issued under Article 36 of 發展觀光條例 (Tourism Development Act). Originally promulgated 2004-02-11; most recent amendment **2024-04-16** (交授觀景字第11340006001號, amending Articles 16–19, adding free-diving) [[全國法規資料庫](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=k0110024)] [[Admin.Taiwan.net.tw](https://admin.taiwan.net.tw/BusinessInfo/Articles?a=1603)] (accessed 2026-04-21).

**Managing authority:** 交通部觀光署 (Tourism Administration, MOTC) at the national level, with day-to-day supervision delegated to **directly-governed municipalities and county (city) governments** (Article 4). Each county can designate/ban specific rivers and add local rules.

**溯溪 status — important clarification:**
The 水域遊憩活動管理辦法 itself lists specific activities (水上摩托車, 潛水, 獨木舟, 立式划槳, SUP, 自由潛水 added 2024, etc.), but **溯溪 is governed primarily by a separate instrument: "辦理溯溪活動應注意事項"** issued by the Sports Administration (體育署, now 運動部) under 教育部 [[教育部主管法規共用系統](https://edu.law.moe.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=GL001979)] (accessed 2026-04-21).

**Open question — confirm with lawyer:** When a county government (e.g., Yilan, Hualien) has designated 溯溪 as a managed water activity under Article 4 of 水域遊憩活動管理辦法 by local ordinance, the 辦法's general safety provisions (Article 9: insurance for operators; Article 10: safety briefings) apply directly. Coverage depends on county. **Assume applicable in all three counties under consideration.**

### Key Operator Obligations under 辦理溯溪活動應注意事項

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Pre-notification | Submit activity plan to **county/city authority at least 14 days in advance** |
| Guide-to-client ratio | ≤5 participants → 1 guide; ≤10 → 2; ≤15 → 3 (i.e., **1:5**) |
| Mandatory gear | Helmet, PFD (life jacket), river-tracing shoes, GPS, comms (mobile/radio/sat phone based on signal); if river segment >500m, flotation device may substitute PFD |
| Guide qualification | **溯溪嚮導** (holder of 山域嚮導 — 溯溪 category cert) OR a "安全人員" holding a recognized canyoning safety cert from domestic/foreign body |
| Insurance | Operator must carry activity-participant insurance |

Source: [辦理溯溪活動應注意事項](https://edu.law.moe.gov.tw/LawContent.aspx?id=GL001979) (accessed 2026-04-21).

## 2. Guide Certifications

### 2.1 溯溪嚮導 — 山域嚮導資格檢定辦法 (LEGALLY REQUIRED for commercial 溯溪)

- **Authority:** 運動部 (Sports Ministry; previously 教育部體育署).
- **Legal basis:** 山域嚮導資格檢定辦法.
- **Minimum age:** 18. Clean criminal record, physical exam, BLS/CPR cert required.
- **Training:** **≥48 hours** combining classroom + field. Written + practical exam.
- **2025 exam sessions:** written 4/11, 7/11, 10/31 [[Adventure Taiwan](https://www.adventuretaiwan.com/mountain-guide-program/canyoning-guide/)] (accessed 2026-04-21).
- **Cost:** Private provider training runs roughly **NT$25,000–45,000** per person (market rate for 48-hr programs); government exam fees modest (~NT$2,000).
- **Ratio limit (day trip):** **1 guide : 8 clients max** under the 辦法注意事項 for 溪降 specialty. (Stricter 1:5 applies under 辦理溯溪活動應注意事項 — the stricter rule governs.)

**Conclusion:** At least one Taiwanese founder should hold this cert before launch. **P0.**

### 2.2 紅十字會 水上安全救生員 (INDUSTRY STANDARD, not strictly required but expected)

- **Authority:** 中華民國紅十字會.
- **Cost:** ~NT$6,000–10,000 (varies by branch).
- **Duration:** ~40 hours.
- Source: [紅十字會水上安全救生員訓練辦法 PDF](https://www.redcross.org.tw/userfiles/files/W01%E6%B0%B4%E4%B8%8A%E5%AE%89%E5%85%A8%E6%95%91%E7%94%9F%E5%93%A1%E8%A8%93%E7%B7%B4%E8%BE%A6%E6%B3%95.pdf) [[Red Cross Class Portal](https://www.redcross-class.org.tw/ClassInfo)] (accessed 2026-04-21).
- **Recommendation:** Strongly recommended for all guides. Boosts insurance underwriting and client trust. **P1.**

### 2.3 International canyoning certifications (CIC, ACA, Rescue 3)

- **CIC (Commission Internationale de Canyon) / ACA / Rescue 3** are *not* formally recognized under Taiwan's 山域嚮導資格檢定辦法, but may qualify a holder as a "安全人員" under 辦理溯溪活動應注意事項 (which accepts "certificates issued by domestic or foreign legal entities, groups, or organizations … whose business is canyoning safety supervision, management, or instruction").
- **Open question — confirm with lawyer:** Whether a foreign cert alone is sufficient for a *lead guide* (嚮導) role vs. only a secondary 安全人員 role. Practical reading: **lead must be TW 溯溪嚮導; internationals certs supplement.**
- **Costs:** Rescue 3 SRT (Swiftwater Rescue Technician) ~USD $700–900 / ~NT$22–28k (3 days); CIC canyon modules typically EUR 500+ per level.

### 2.4 Other TW industry orgs

- **中華民國山岳協會** — issues mountaineering certs; not canyoning-specific.
- **中華民國水中運動協會 (CMAS TW)** — runs 溯溪訓練 programs [[cmas.tw](http://www.cmas.tw/modules/tinyd2/index.php?id=8)].
- **TW Outdoor Guide Association / Adventure Taiwan / 冒險精靈** — private training providers that prep for the 運動部 exam.

## 3. Company Registration

### 3.1 有限公司 vs 行號

| | 有限公司 (Ltd.) | 行號 (Sole Prop / Partnership) |
|---|---|---|
| Liability | Limited to capital | **Unlimited personal** |
| Minimum capital | No statutory minimum; **NT$500k recommended** for foreign-manager work permit pathway | No minimum |
| Foreign shareholder | Allowed, 100% OK | Allowed but restrictive for foreigners |
| Tax | Corporate 20% + dividend | Flow-through to personal |
| Best fit | Anything with injury risk, multiple owners, third-party bookings | Tiny solo operations |

Sources: [TECL Taipei Entrepreneur Center (2025)](https://www.tecl.com.tw/en/articles/how-do-foreigners-set-up-a-company-in-taiwan-qa/); [StartABusinessInTaiwan.tw](https://startabusinessintaiwan.tw/book/faqs/setting-up-a-business/is-there-a-minimum-capital-requirement-for-setting-up-a-business-in-taiwan/) (accessed 2026-04-21).

**Recommendation: 有限公司.** Outdoor adventure liability exposure makes 行號 reckless despite budget.

### 3.2 Can the US founder be a shareholder/director without a TW visa?

**Yes, as a shareholder and director — no resident director required.** A foreign individual can hold 100% of a TW 有限公司; no TW residency is required merely to be a shareholder or listed director [[Fidinam](https://www.fidinam.com/en/set-up-company-in-taiwan)] [[wfoe-accounting.com Q&A](https://www.wfoe-accounting.com/qa-about-setting-up-foreign-owned-limited-liability-company-in-taiwan)] (accessed 2026-04-21).

However:
- Because he is a **foreign investor**, his investment triggers **Foreign Investment Approval (FIA)** through the **Department of Investment Review (投資審議司, formerly 投審會 / now under MOEA DIR)**. FIA is a prerequisite to register the capital inflow. [[ICLG FDI Taiwan 2026](https://iclg.com/practice-areas/foreign-direct-investment-regimes-laws-and-regulations/taiwan)] [[MOEA DIR](https://www.moea.gov.tw/Mns/dir_e/Investment/DirApply_En.aspx?kind=A&subkind=01&menu_id=42924)] (accessed 2026-04-21).
- Timeline: typically 2–6 weeks; no filing fee.
- If he wants to **work / draw a salary** in Taiwan, he'd need a work permit (which then needs NT$500k paid-in capital and foreign ownership ≥1/3). Being a passive shareholder/director does not require this.

**Open question — confirm with lawyer:** Whether structuring him as a minority (<1/3) investor simplifies compliance. Also confirm whether FIA is still required under the newer negative-list approach in tourism/recreation sector.

## 4. Insurance

### 4.1 旅行業責任保險 — mandatory ONLY for licensed travel agencies

Under 旅行業管理規則 Art. 53, licensed 旅行業 must carry liability insurance with minimum coverage: **NT$2.5M–5M per-person accidental death, NT$100k–200k medical** (ranges reflect different tour types — domestic/inbound/outbound) [[交通部觀光署](https://admin.taiwan.net.tw/BusinessInfo/Insurance?a=220)] [[風險管理與保險教育推廣入口網](https://rm.ib.gov.tw/pages/Insu.aspx?Dir=013000)] (accessed 2026-04-21).

Note: Some sources quote figures in the "百萬" range that appear to conflate tour-type tiers. Confirm exact tier with underwriter.

**This does NOT apply to us if we do not hold a travel-agency license.** It DOES apply to any travel agency we partner with — they must insure their clients on our trips.

### 4.2 Public liability insurance (公共意外責任保險) — what we actually need

- Standard for any activity provider. Typical coverage: NT$3M–5M per incident, NT$10M aggregate.
- **Typical premium for small outdoor operators:** NT$20,000–60,000/year, rising with head-count and activity risk.
- Known underwriters with river-tracing appetite: **新光產險, 富邦產險, 旺旺友聯 [[wwunion.com](https://www.wwunion.com/?page_id=3578)]**, 國泰產險 (accessed 2026-04-21).
- **Open question — confirm with broker:** Whether carriers exclude 溯溪/canyoning without cert-of-guide documentation. Expect exclusions unless 嚮導 certs are named insureds.

### 4.3 Participant accident insurance

Supplemental per-participant coverage (typically NT$20–80 per person per day for NT$2M coverage). Easy to bolt on. **P0 for launch.**

## 5. Travel Agency License (旅行業執照)

### 5.1 Capital requirements

| Type | Paid-in capital | Each branch | Bond (保證金) | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 綜合 (Comprehensive) | **NT$30M** | +1.5M | NT$10M | All, incl. packaging wholesale |
| 甲種 (Class A) | **NT$6M** | +1M | NT$1.5M | **Inbound foreigners + outbound** |
| 乙種 (Class B) | **NT$1.2M** | +600k | NT$600k | Domestic TW tours only |

Sources: [誠樸會計師](https://ppcpa.expert/docs/startups/special/travelagency/); [交通部觀光署分類](https://admin.taiwan.net.tw/BusinessInfo/Articles?a=219); [旅行業管理規則](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=K0110002) (accessed 2026-04-21).

**丙種 does not exist** in current regulation. Classification is 綜合 / 甲種 / 乙種.

### 5.2 Can day-trip activity providers sell directly to foreigners WITHOUT this license?

**This is the single most important legal question for your business.** The conservative legal reading is:
- Selling a **packaged itinerary including transport + activity + anything that looks like a tour** to foreign tourists triggers 旅行業 classification → **甲種 license required (NT$6M capital)** which is **well beyond your NT$100k budget**.
- Selling a **standalone activity experience** (client self-transports to trailhead, signs up for a "canyoning class/experience," pays for the activity only) is widely treated in practice as an "體驗活動" or sports/instruction service — not travel-agency business.

In practice: most TW canyoning outfits (SHAWA, Taiwan Canyoning, Adventure Taiwan) operate as activity providers, NOT as travel agencies. They list on **KKday / Klook / Niceday**, which themselves are (or partner with) licensed agencies and handle the "selling tours to foreigners" piece legally.

**Open question — confirm with lawyer:** The exact line. In particular: (a) whether offering hotel pickup, (b) whether multi-day bundling with lodging, triggers 旅行業 classification.

### 5.3 The standard workaround: partnership with licensed agency

**Model:** You operate as an activity provider (NT$100k 有限公司). You list on KKday / Klook / a partner TW 甲種 agency. They collect payment from foreign tourists, issue the invoice, and remit minus commission (typically **15–25%**) to you. They also carry the mandatory 旅行業責任保險 on bookings made through them.

This is the dominant model for small adventure operators in Taiwan and is the **recommended path given your budget and timeline**.

## 6. County-Specific Access

### 6.1 Hualien / Taroko

- **Taroko National Park entry:** free; no general entry permit. Restricted trails (e.g., Zhuilu Old Trail) require permits via the unified [hike.taiwan.gov.tw](https://hike.taiwan.gov.tw/) portal [[Taiwan Adventures](https://www.taiwan-adventures.com/how-to-apply-for-hiking-permits-in-taroko-national-park/)] (accessed 2026-04-21).
- **Post-2024 earthquake status (as of April 2026):** Many trails/sections remain closed or restricted; reopening has been staged [[tttrips.com 2025 guide](https://tttrips.com/en/article/443)] (accessed 2026-04-21).
- **Canyoning in Taroko gorge proper:** heavily restricted; commercial 溯溪 largely operates on rivers OUTSIDE the park boundary (e.g., 三棧南溪, 慕谷慕魚, though the latter also has access politics). **Open question — confirm current list of open rivers with Hualien county govt.**

### 6.2 Yilan

- **南澳 (Nanao)** is the epicenter of commercial 溯溪 in Taiwan: 鹿皮溪 (Lu-Pi / Deer-skin Creek), 金岳瀑布, 無名溪 are the bread-and-butter day-trip routes [[Klook Nanao](https://www.klook.com/en-US/activity/12386-river-tracing-rock-diving-experience-jinyue-waterfall-yilan/)] [[SHAWA Lu-Pi page](https://www.shawatw.com/en/activity/%E9%B9%BF%E7%9A%AE%E6%BA%AA/)] (accessed 2026-04-21).
- **哈盆 (Hapen):** lies across the Yilan/New Taipei border in the 哈盆自然保留區 — a designated **Nature Reserve** under the Cultural Heritage Preservation Act. **Commercial tours generally prohibited** without scientific research permit. Treat as off-limits. **Open question — verify with Forestry Agency.**
- **Seasonal:** Typhoon-season closures (roughly June–October) are enforced ad-hoc by Nanao Township and the fire department. No single published calendar; operators self-regulate via weather + water-level checks.

### 6.3 Pingtung / Maolin

- **Maolin 茂林** is the traditional territory of the **Rukai (魯凱族)** — specifically Wanshan, Tona, and Maya communities [[Maolin NSA](https://theme.maolin-nsa.gov.tw/kids/page-4_1.html)] (accessed 2026-04-21).
- **Access:** Some zones are on 原住民保留地 (indigenous reserved land) and require **village-council consultation / entry fees**. Permits administered via [hike.taiwan.gov.tw](https://hike.taiwan.gov.tw/) for mountain-entry areas; separate informal community agreements for specific canyons.
- **Best rivers for canyoning:** 茂林溪, 濁口溪 tributaries. Less commercial density than Nanao — opportunity AND higher relationship-building cost.

### 6.4 Which is easiest for a new entrant?

**Ranking:**
1. **Yilan (南澳)** — easiest. Established commercial corridor, existing operators, clear routes, proximity to Taipei = steady foreign tourist flow via Klook/KKday. **Recommended base.**
2. **Pingtung (Maolin)** — moderate. Less competition but requires 6–12 months of indigenous-community relationship building before commercial operations. Potential differentiator if done right.
3. **Hualien** — hardest right now due to post-earthquake access uncertainty in Taroko region, though brand-value is highest long-term.

## 7. Recommended Legal Path

**Structure:**

1. **Entity:** **有限公司** registered in Yilan with **NT$500,000 paid-in capital** (3 TW founders 70%, US founder 30%). Keeps US founder below the 1/3 threshold that triggers more complex work-permit implications.
2. **FIA filing** via MOEA Dept. of Investment Review for the US founder's share. Budget ~NT$15k lawyer fee.
3. **Licensing:** **NO travel-agency license.** Operate as activity provider only.
4. **Sales channel:** Partner with **KKday + Klook + 1 licensed TW 甲種 agency** for foreign-tourist bookings. They handle legal sale + 旅行業責任保險.
5. **Guides:** At least 1 Taiwanese founder obtains **山域嚮導 — 溯溪類** cert (P0, pre-launch). All guides obtain **紅十字會 水上安全救生員** + CPR/BLS. Ideally ≥1 has **Rescue 3 SRT**.
6. **Insurance:** **公共意外責任保險** NT$5M/NT$10M aggregate (~NT$30–50k/yr) + per-participant accident rider. Confirm carrier explicitly covers 溯溪.
7. **Operations:** File 14-day-advance 實施計畫 with Yilan County Government for each activity batch (can be standing plan for regular routes).
8. **Target:** Launch with 1–2 signature Nanao day trips (鹿皮溪 beginner, 金岳 waterfall intermediate). Book exclusively via partner platforms for first 12 months.

**Estimated startup cost vs NT$100k budget:**

| Line item | NT$ |
|---|---|
| Company registration + FIA (legal fees) | 20,000 |
| Paid-in capital (recoverable working capital) | 500,000 (separate from 100k budget) |
| 溯溪嚮導 cert x 1 | 35,000 |
| 水上安全救生員 x 2 | 15,000 |
| Public liability insurance (year 1) | 35,000 |
| Initial gear top-up, website, misc. | 25,000 |
| **Total (ex-capital)** | **~130,000** |

**This is ~NT$30k over the NT$100k budget.** Options to close gap: (a) defer one救生員 cert to year 2; (b) founders contribute gear; (c) use Klook's sliding insurance rider instead of annual policy Year 1.

## 8. Open Questions — For Lawyer / Consultant Call

1. **Exact boundary:** At what point does offering a "溯溪 experience" become "旅行業" under 發展觀光條例 Art. 27? (hotel pickup? multi-day? bundled meals?)
2. **FIA:** Is tourism/recreation on the current negative list for foreign investment, or is FIA a simple notification?
3. **Sports-sector exemption:** Does the **運動部 "山域嚮導" regulatory regime** preempt/overlap with 交通部觀光署's 水域遊憩活動管理辦法 for 溯溪? Which filing dominates?
4. **County ordinances:** Has Yilan formally designated 溯溪 as a managed activity under Art. 4 of 水域遊憩活動管理辦法? What local supplemental rules apply?
5. **International certs:** Will running a foreign-cert-only US founder as a secondary guide (安全人員) satisfy the headcount rules?
6. **Insurance wording:** Confirm that 公共意外責任保險 with carriers named above explicitly covers canyoning/溯溪 without rider exclusions.
7. **Partner-agency contract:** Template terms when listing as a product on KKday/Klook — who holds risk for what, how insurance claims flow.
8. **Nanao land use:** Are 鹿皮溪 and 金岳 trailheads on 林班地 / 原住民保留地 — do we need Forestry Agency or community agreements?
9. **Tax/invoicing:** GUI / 統一發票 issuance for foreign-tourist bookings routed through partner agencies vs. direct.
10. **Employment:** If the 3 TW founders wear guide hats while being shareholders, any labor-law issues under 勞基法 (worker accident insurance obligations when guiding)?
